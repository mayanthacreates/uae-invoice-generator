UAE VAT invoicing, answered

What must a UAE tax invoice include? +

Under FTA rules a tax invoice must show the words "Tax Invoice", your business name, address and TRN, the client's details, a sequential invoice number, the issue date, a description of each supply, the amount payable, and the VAT charged at 5%. This generator lays all of that out for you.

Do I need to show my TRN on every invoice? +

Yes. If you are VAT-registered in the UAE, your 15-digit Tax Registration Number must appear on every tax invoice you issue. If your client is also VAT-registered, it's good practice to include their TRN too.

What is the VAT rate in the UAE? +

The standard UAE VAT rate is 5%, applied to most goods and services. Some supplies are zero-rated or exempt - if that applies to you, switch the VAT toggle off and issue a standard invoice instead.

Can I invoice without being VAT-registered? +

Yes. Businesses below the mandatory AED 375,000 registration threshold can issue regular invoices without VAT. Turn the 5% VAT toggle off and leave the TRN field empty - the document will render as a plain invoice.
